invokr

Sends the songs you like on YouTube Music to your local archive server.
Find it in server/config.json as the token field.
Requires Firefox 149+; on older builds it quietly falls back to badging the toolbar icon. Matches the server is confident about are downloaded without asking at all.
The popup shows a badge on the toolbar icon when a review is waiting. The tab option is easier for long lists and the artwork picker.
